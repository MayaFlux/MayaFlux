void MayaFlux::Nodes::Network::WalkOperator::process ( float  frames)
overridevirtual

Self-contained step.

Does nothing when every_n_blocks is 0 (the default). Otherwise counts blocks and calls advance() every every_n_blocks calls, so the chain progresses on its own with no code outside the network. Runs regardless of whether advance() is also being called externally; the two do not conflict, they simply both move the same walk.
