Weighted sum wrapped into [0, modulus).

coefs: [modulus, weights...]. In a recursive Polynomial the weights act on the last outputs, which is a lagged-Fibonacci style recurrence with the structure the weights and modulus give it. A modulus of zero or less leaves the sum unwrapped.
