void MayaFlux::Buffers::RenderProcessor::execute_shader ( const std::shared_ptr< VKBuffer > &  buffer)
overrideprotectedvirtual

Records and registers a secondary command buffer every call, even with nothing to draw (a GraphicsOperator with zero items, or a triangulated span nothing was milled from this cycle).

RootGraphicsBuffer's present pass only acquires/presents a window once at least one of its buffers has a valid recorded command buffer; a buffer that never registers one (because it always bailed out early while empty) leaves that window permanently unpresented, not merely blank, for as long as it stays empty.

Implements MayaFlux::Buffers::ShaderProcessor.
