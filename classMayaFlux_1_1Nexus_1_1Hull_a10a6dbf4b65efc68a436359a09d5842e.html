void MayaFlux::Nexus::Hull::refit ( )

Read the buffer's geometry again and take its bounds.

For a deforming mesh or a drifting cloud. Blocks for a ComputeMeshBuffer or a buffer a GpuFieldOperator drives, so call it off the graphics thread. The previous bounds are kept if nothing can be read.
