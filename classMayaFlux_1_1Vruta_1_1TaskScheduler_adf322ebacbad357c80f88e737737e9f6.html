void MayaFlux::Vruta::TaskScheduler::terminate_all_tasks ( )

Terminate and clear all tasks.

Submissions made while this runs, including from completion callbacks, are rejected. Must not be called from the conditional worker thread or from a callback fired during a queue drain; such calls are ignored. New tasks are accepted again once this returns, or if any step throws.
