28/**
29 * @class Chimera
30 * @brief Assembles the layers of a TextureArrayBuffer from whatever feeds them, so a
31 * fragment shader can build one picture out of many.
32 *
33 * A layer holds a still image, a GPU image copied every frame, or a moment of a ring
34 * or a texture collection that is still being recorded. Moments are read at a lag
35 * behind the write head, with
36 * a lag that varies in time, or played freely from where the layer entered, forward,
37 * held or backward, optionally smoothed between frames. A layer can be cut to
38 * another moment, or given a new source, while running. Layers are numbered in
39 * declaration order, enter after their own delay, and refresh every frame or every
40 * few. Copies share the same layers, and the set stops when the last copy goes.
41 *
42 * A lagged layer stays at least two frames behind the frame being written. A layer
43 * whose position falls outside what the ring still holds keeps its last frame and
44 * reports once. stop() holds every layer on its last frame.
45 *
46 * Built from a buffer with ChimeraBuilder or create_chimera. The layers are fed by
47 * the cycles of the builder's pipeline, one per frame, after whatever the pipeline
48 * records. What the layers mean on screen is the buffer's fragment shader. Call the
49 * controls from the frame clock.
50 */

163/**
164 * @class ChimeraBuilder
165 * @brief Declares the layers of a Chimera, in order, and starts feeding them.
166 *
167 * layer() opens a new layer and the calls after it describe that layer. A call made
168 * before any layer() describes the first.
169 *
170 * @code{.cpp}
171 * auto chimera = MayaFlux::create_chimera(array)
172 * .layer().from("res/eye.png").params({ 0.3F, 0.4F, 0.2F, 0.F })
173 * .layer().from("res/mouth.png").params({ 0.5F, 0.7F, 0.3F, 0.F })
174 * .layer().from(ring).lag(0.0)
175 * .layer().from(ring).lag(0.5).smooth().level(0.6)
176 * .start();
177 * @endcode
178 *
179 * The builder holds one BufferPipeline, the one it was given or one it was made
180 * with, so what is recorded and what is assembled read as one chain:
181 *
182 * @code{.cpp}
183 * auto chimera = MayaFlux::create_chimera(array)
184 * .record(BufferOperation::capture_to_stream(io, camera, 240))
185 * .layer().from_pipeline().lag(0.0)
186 * .layer().from_pipeline().lag(0.5)
187 * .start();
188 * @endcode
189 */
