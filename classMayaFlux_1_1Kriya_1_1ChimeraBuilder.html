Detailed Description

Declares the layers of a Chimera, in order, and starts feeding them.

layer() opens a new layer and the calls after it describe that layer. A call made before any layer() describes the first.

auto chimera = MayaFlux::create_chimera(array)
.layer().from("res/eye.png").params({ 0.3F, 0.4F, 0.2F, 0.F })
.layer().from("res/mouth.png").params({ 0.5F, 0.7F, 0.3F, 0.F })
.layer().from(ring).lag(0.0)
.layer().from(ring).lag(0.5).smooth().level(0.6)
.start();

The builder holds one BufferPipeline, the one it was given or one it was made with, so what is recorded and what is assembled read as one chain:

auto chimera = MayaFlux::create_chimera(array)
.record(BufferOperation::capture_to_stream(io, camera, 240))
.layer().from_pipeline().lag(0.0)
.layer().from_pipeline().lag(0.5)
.start();
