9 * @brief Mutable insertion adapter for a CompositeArray.
10 *
11 * Appends empty elements, writes fields by name, and copies compatible
12 * Composite values from another array. Copying an element also copies its
13 * present text values and rebases their offsets into the destination's text
14 * store. The adapter borrows its destination array and does not own storage.
15 *
16 * Usage:
17 * @code
18 * CompositeInsertion insert(items);
19 * auto index = insert.append();
20 * insert.set_text(index, "name", "Ada");
21 * insert.set(index, "score", 0.95);
22 * @endcode
23 *
24 * Copying between arrays with the same finalized layout:
25 * @code
26 * CompositeInsertion destination_insert(destination);

29 * @endcode
