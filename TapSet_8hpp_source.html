80/**
81 * @class TapSetBuilder
82 * @brief Describes a tap set one tap at a time, then starts it.
83 *
84 * Each tap() begins a tap with defaults: enters at once, normal speed, forward,
85 * full level, output channel 0. The calls that follow describe that tap until
86 * the next tap(). Calling one before any tap() starts the first. loop() and
87 * region() apply to every tap.
88 *
89 * @code
90 * auto taps = MayaFlux::create_tap_set("res/h.wav")
91 * .tap().speed(1.0)
92 * .tap().enters_after(2.0).speed(3.0 / 2.0).level(0.8)
93 * .tap().enters_after(4.0).speed(2.0).backward().level(0.6).on_channels({ 0, 1 })
94 * .start();
95 *
96 * taps.speed(0, 0.5);
97 * taps.stop();
98 * @endcode
99 *
100 * Two taps at speeds 1.0 and 1.001 drift slowly out of phase. Speeds change
101 * pitch, since taps read with interpolation.
102 *
103 * A tap plays the source channel with the same number as each output channel,
104 * wrapping when the source has fewer, so a mono source sounds on every output
105 * and a stereo one keeps left and right. from_channel() picks one source
106 * channel for all of a tap's outputs.
107 */
