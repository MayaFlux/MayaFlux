enum class MayaFlux::Kinesis::Vision::VisionIntent : uint32_t
strong

Composable flags naming what a vision analysis should produce.

Backed by today's VisionOp catalog: FindElements, TrackObjects, DetectFeatures, DetectEdges, EstimateMotion, MeasureAppearance.

Named but not yet backed by any VisionOp; resolving one of these is expected to fail clearly until a supporting op exists, the same way VisionGpuExecutor::config() returns INVALID_SHADER for an op with no GPU implementation rather than omitting the op: Classify, EstimateDepth, DetectAnomaly, Register.

Enumerator
NONE 
FindElements 

Localize discrete things in the frame (thresholding/morphology into ConnectedComponents/FindContours).

TrackObjects 

Maintain identity of specific points across frames (ExtractPeaks into TrackKeypoints).

DetectFeatures 

Distinctive points with no persistence claim (HarrisResponse into ExtractPeaks alone).

DetectEdges 

Boundary/gradient detection (Sobel, Scharr, Canny).

EstimateMotion 

Dense, whole-field motion with no identity claim (OpticalFlowDense).

Kept separate from TrackObjects: a global flow field and a persistently tracked point are different claims.

MeasureAppearance 

Photometric/statistical characterization of the frame itself, independent of anything found in it.

Classify 

Assign a label to a detected region or the whole frame.

No backing VisionOp exists yet; needs a learned-model op.

EstimateDepth 

Per-pixel depth or disparity.

No backing VisionOp exists yet; a different output shape entirely from every flag above it.

DetectAnomaly 

Flag departure from a learned or authored baseline.

No backing VisionOp exists yet; needs a reference/baseline concept VisionOp has no notion of today.

Register 

Align two or more frames into a common reference.

No backing VisionOp exists yet; the only flag here whose input is plural.
