Convenience overload of backward_difference<N> over a raw span.

Evaluated directly on the span, newest sample first, without building a HistoryBuffer, so it does not allocate and is safe per sample on the audio thread. Samples beyond the end of a span shorter than N + 1 read as zero, the same initial condition a HistoryBuffer starts from.
