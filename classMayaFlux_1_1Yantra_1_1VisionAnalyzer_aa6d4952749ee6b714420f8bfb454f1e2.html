std::optional< Kinesis::Vision::VisionAnalysis > MayaFlux::Yantra::VisionAnalyzer::advance ( const std::shared_ptr< Core::VKImage > &  image)

Advance an analysis of image by one sequence per call, without waiting on deferred GPU work.

The first call copies image and starts a run on the copy. Later calls continue that run and ignore image until it completes. The flow steps of TrackObjects and EstimateMotion are submitted deferred and polled on later calls; other steps still wait inline.

Returns
The analysis once every sequence has completed, otherwise nullopt.
