Detailed Description

Owns a sequence of values that share a CompositeLayout.

Owns two uint8_t DataVariants: fixed-stride packed elements and variable- length UTF-8 text. The layout carries field names, types, offsets, and stride; the presence bitmap distinguishes absent fields from zero or empty values.

Usage:

CompositeLayout layout;
layout.add_field<std::string>("name");
layout.add_field<double>("score");
CompositeArray items(std::move(layout));
auto index = items.append();
items.set_text(index, "name", "Ada");
items.set(index, "score", 0.95);
auto item = items.at(index);
