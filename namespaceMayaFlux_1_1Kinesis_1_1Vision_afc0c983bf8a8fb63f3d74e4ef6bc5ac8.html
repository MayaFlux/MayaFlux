inline

Place up to requested pyramid levels for a w x h base image.

Levels stop early when the next one would have a side under min_extent, so small images get fewer levels rather than degenerate ones. requested is clamped to [1, PyramidLayout::k_max_levels].

Parameters
wBase width in pixels.
hBase height in pixels.
requestedDesired number of levels including the base.
min_extentSmallest side a level may have.
Returns
Layout with level 0 at the origin and the rest stacked to its right.
