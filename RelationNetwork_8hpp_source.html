10/**
11 * @class RelationNetwork
12 * @brief Host for a dynamically growable set of slots and the operators that act on them
13 *
14 * The network owns the slots; everything computational lives in operators:
15 * advancing slot signals (AdvanceOperator), deriving one slot from others
16 * (DeriveOperator), turning slots into published outputs (CombineOperator),
17 * and any other slot logic (StepOperator and operators built on the same
18 * base). Construction adds no operators and no slots; a network with no
19 * publishing operator outputs nothing.
20 *
21 * Add slots with add_slot(), operators with add<Op>(...) in this order:
22 * control operators first, then AdvanceOperator, then DeriveOperator, then
23 * CombineOperator.
24 *
25 * @warning A RelationSlot's address is not stable: add_slot() may reallocate
26 * the whole slot vector. Read a slot fresh through get_slot(index)
27 * or get_node_output(index) rather than holding a pointer to it.
28 *
29 * add_slot() reserves each new slot's block storage, so a slot added while
30 * the network is already running does not allocate on its first block.
31 * process_batch() re-supplies every operator the current slot storage,
32 * applies mapped parameters, and runs the chain in order. Output 0 of the
33 * first publishing operator becomes the network's audio buffer.
34 *
35 * Structural edits (add_slot(), slot sources, callbacks, adding operators)
36 * must happen outside process_batch(). Parameter mapping uses Source and
37 * map() on RelationOperator (see RelationOperators.hpp).
38 */
