10/**
11 * @class VisionMatrix
12 * @brief Compute matrix holding one VisionAnalyzer for a query and, when an
13 * extraction mode is given, one VisionExtractor.
14 *
15 * An intent set in the query without its parameters runs with the defaults.
16 * Keep the matrix for as long as frames arrive: its analyzer reuses its GPU
17 * images on every call, and tracking and motion compare each frame with the
18 * previous one. Configure the extraction through extractor(). Build it with
19 * create(): the matrix must be held by a shared_ptr to run.
20 *
21 * An analysis can be extracted from any frame, not only the one it was taken
22 * on. Boxes, contours, tracks, keypoints and appearance statistics are
23 * normalized values held on the CPU: they stay valid after later calls and
24 * apply at any size. The edge and flow images, and the labels mask() reads,
25 * belong to the analyzer and are valid only until its next call.
26 */
