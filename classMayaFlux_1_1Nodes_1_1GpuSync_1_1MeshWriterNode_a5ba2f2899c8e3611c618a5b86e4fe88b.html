std::optional< Kakshya::MeshData > MayaFlux::Nodes::GpuSync::MeshWriterNode::get_mesh_data ( ) const

The node's current geometry as MeshData, the inverse of set_mesh(const MeshData&).

Vertices and indices are copied as stored, in the node's own space with no transform applied. The canonical mesh layout is used.

Returns
The mesh, or nullopt when the node has no vertices or no indices.
