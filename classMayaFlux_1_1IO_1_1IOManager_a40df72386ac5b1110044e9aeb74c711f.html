void MayaFlux::IO::IOManager::stop_spatial_capture ( uint32_t  capture_id)

Stop a running spatial capture and release it.

Frames already written are kept. The underlying archive stays open and registered under its filepath as long as any other capture still shares it. No-op with a warning if the id is unknown.
