9/**
10 * @struct ColorPicker
11 * @brief A saturation and value square, a hue strip and a swatch, drawn into one buffer.
12 *
13 * Configure with the setters, then place() once. After place(), picker_id,
14 * color, hsv, picker_bounds and cursor_out are populated. Pressing or dragging
15 * the square sets saturation and value, and the strip sets hue. Hue is kept
16 * in hsv, so it survives picking a grey or black. color holds the same value
17 * as red, green and blue, each in [0, 1].
18 *
19 * color is registered with the Bridge, so Forma::bridge().at(color).write(sink)
20 * receives three floats; hsv is registered the same way and receives hue,
21 * saturation and value. A Bridge inbound write changes the value but does not
22 * move the markers. Forma::destroy(surface, picker_id) removes the picker.
23 *
24 * @code
25 * auto picker = ColorPicker {}
26 * .initial({ 0.9F, 0.4F, 0.1F })
27 * .on_change([](glm::vec3 rgb) { })
28 * .place(surface, cursor, 0.4F);
29 * @endcode
30 */
