Extract contours from an existing CPU component labeling.

Applies the same tracing, filtering, and measurement rules as mask-based extraction without repeating connected-component labeling.

Parameters
componentsCPU labeling with a complete w * h label_map and compact foreground labels in 1..count; zero labels are background.
wImage width in pixels.
hImage height in pixels.
min_areaMinimum normalised area to retain.
max_contoursMaximum number of contours to return; zero is unlimited.
max_points_per_contourMaximum trace length; zero is unlimited.
Returns
Outer and hole contours with normalized points and pixel perimeter.
