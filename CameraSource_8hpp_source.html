9/**
10 * @class CameraSource
11 * @brief Abstract interface for a live camera backend hosted by IOManager.
12 *
13 * Covers only the post-open lifecycle: negotiated parameters, frame
14 * delivery, and IOService wiring. Deliberately excludes device opening and
15 * configuration — those are backend-specific by nature, and forcing them
16 * into a shared signature would constrain every future backend to one
17 * config shape. A concrete backend owns its own open()-equivalent and is
18 * handed to IOManager already open, via IOManager::register_camera_source().
19 *
20 * A camera source is a live, unbounded stream: no ring buffer, no seek, no
21 * batch decode. One frame is pulled per process cycle, demand-driven by
22 * CameraContainer::process_default(), never pushed speculatively ahead of
23 * the consumer.
24 *
25 * Two integration paths apply to any implementation:
26 * - Managed: IOManager owns construction and opening (backend-specific),
27 * then calls register_camera_source(), which assigns a
28 * reader_id and wires the container's IOService callback.
29 * - Standalone: the caller drives the same sequence manually —
30 * create_container() → setup_io_service(id) →
31 * set_container() → close() — without IOManager.
32 *
33 * FFmpegCameraReader is the first concrete implementation.
34 */
