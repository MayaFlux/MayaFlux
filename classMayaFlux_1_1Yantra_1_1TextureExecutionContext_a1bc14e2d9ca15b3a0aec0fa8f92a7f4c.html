output_type MayaFlux::Yantra::TextureExecutionContext::execute ( const input_type &  input,
const ExecutionContext &  ctx 
)
inlineoverridevirtual

Override for CHAINED mode: skips collect_gpu_outputs().

Callers driving multi-pass image dispatch (e.g. ConnectedComponents merge loop) may flip an image slot to INPUT_OUTPUT for the cross-pass barrier in dispatch_batched, which leaves no OUTPUT-direction slot for collect_gpu_outputs' output_slot() lookup to find. Non-CHAINED modes are unaffected and fall through to the base implementation.

Parameters
inputIgnored for CHAINED mode (image already staged manually).
ctxExecutionContext; CHAINED mode requires pass_count and pc_updater.
