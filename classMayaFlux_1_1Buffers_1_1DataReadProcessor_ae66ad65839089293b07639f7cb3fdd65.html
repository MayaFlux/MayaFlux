void MayaFlux::Buffers::DataReadProcessor::read_mesh ( )

Read the attached mesh.

A MeshBuffer, a GeometryBuffer driven by a MeshWriterNode, and a MeshNetworkBuffer yield a copy of their CPU-authoritative geometry with no transfer; a network is merged into one mesh with a submesh per slot, in local space with no transforms applied. A ComputeMeshBuffer or a VolumeGridBuffer with a surface yields its live vertices as non-indexed triangles, sized by its live counter rather than its capacity.
