void MayaFlux::IO::IOManager::stop ( )

Finalizes all in-flight output while consumers are still running.

Stops every audio, video, volume and spatial capture through its stop_*_capture() call, closes the sound writers and waits for pending saves. A capture the caller never stopped is still finalized (an Ogawa archive or video container left open is not a valid file), and volume/spatial captures release their scheduler reference here instead of reaching into an already-destroyed scheduler.

The IOService stays registered and readers stay alive, so threads that consume them keep working. Idempotent. Call before the subsystems stop (see Engine::End()), while backend services and logging sinks are alive.
