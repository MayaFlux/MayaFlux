Collapsible & MayaFlux::Portal::Forma::Collapsible::label ( std::string  text)
inline

Composite a text label over the header, background included.

When set, place() presses text with the current open/closed color as Portal::Text::PressParams::background into one texture, and every toggle re-presses it with the new color - background and label are one composited image, not a separate vertex layer, so they can never drift out of sync the way two independent draws could. When empty (default), the header stays a plain color-only quad with no text machinery at all.

buf passed to place() still needs the "text" additional_textures slot when a label is set, same as Element::with_text.
