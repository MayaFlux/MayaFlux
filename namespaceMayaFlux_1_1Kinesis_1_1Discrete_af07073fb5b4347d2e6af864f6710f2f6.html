Ring of logistic maps with nearest neighbour coupling.

Each call maps every cell, f(x) = r x (1 - x), then mixes each cell with its two neighbours (periodic): x' = (1 - eps) f(x) + eps / 2 (f(left) + f(right)). Returns the mean cell minus one half. Uniform cells stay uniform; different cells give the pattern formation the coupling allows.
