bool MayaFlux::Kakshya::TextureCollection::append ( const std::shared_ptr< Core::VKImage > &  image)

Copy image into the next layer.

An image of another size is scaled to fit inside the layer, keeping its aspect ratio, centred on a zeroed background. The format is converted.

Returns
True when the image was stored. False for a null image, when the device cannot blit its format into the collection's, or when a linear collection is full.
