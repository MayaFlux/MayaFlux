Level at a time of a leaky response to a rectangular pulse.

Parameters
tTime
onsetTime the pulse begins
durationLength of the pulse, held to the period when one is given
riseTime constant while the pulse is on; zero or less jumps to one
fallTime constant after the pulse ends; zero or less falls to zero at once
periodRepeat interval; zero or less means a single pulse
Returns
Level in [0, 1]

During the pulse the level climbs toward one as 1 - exp(-s / rise), with s the time since the pulse began. After it ends the level decays from where it stood as exp(-s / fall). Before the onset it is zero. With a period, the pattern repeats from rest every period, so a tail still decaying when the next pulse begins is cut off there.
