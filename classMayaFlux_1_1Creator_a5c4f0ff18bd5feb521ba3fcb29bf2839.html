std::shared_ptr< Kakshya::CameraContainer > MayaFlux::Creator::read_camera ( const IO::CameraConfig &  config)

Open a camera device as a live source container.

Opens via IOManager::open_camera() and returns the container, like read_audio() returns its container. Nothing is hooked until you pipe it into Graphics, or call IOManager::hook_camera_to_buffer().

Parameters
configDevice name, resolution hint, fps hint, format override.
Returns
Opened CameraContainer, or nullptr on failure.
