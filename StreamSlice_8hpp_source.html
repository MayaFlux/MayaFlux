12/**
13 * @struct StreamSlice
14 * @brief A bounded region of a DynamicSoundStream with associated playback parameters.
15 *
16 * Describes a slice of audio data within a DynamicSoundStream as a Region.
17 * region.start_coordinates[0] / end_coordinates[0] are the frame bounds;
18 * region.start_coordinates[1] / end_coordinates[1] are the channel bounds.
19 *
20 * The slice does not own a processor. The region is the contract; whatever
21 * drives this slice resolves the appropriate processor at activation time.
22 * Currently that is CursorAccessProcessor for sequential bounded reads, but
23 * the region descriptor is equally valid input to RegionOrganizationProcessor
24 * (non-linear navigation, selection patterns, transitions) or any future
25 * region-aware processor against a DynamicSoundStream.
26 *
27 * speed and scale are playback parameters applied by the driving processor.
28 * cursor_remainder accumulates sub-frame advancement for speed != 1.0.
29 * looping and index are playback state and identity carried with the slice.
30 *
31 * time_map, when set, replaces speed: it gives the read position in frames as
32 * a function of seconds since the voice was bound (see Kinesis::TimeMaps).
33 * Like the other fields it can be changed while the voice plays.
34 *
35 * source_channel picks which channel of the stream is played. Unset, the voice
36 * plays the stream channel with the same number as its output channel.
37 *
38 * repeat, with a time_map, restarts the map over and over. Its value is how
39 * many seconds each repeat lasts, read when the repeat begins, so a length that
40 * shrinks over time gives a stutter or bouncing repeat.
41 */
