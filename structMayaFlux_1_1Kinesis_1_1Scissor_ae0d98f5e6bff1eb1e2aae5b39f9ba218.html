Project a world-space AABB3D through a ViewTransform and take the NDC bounding box of its projected corners.

Projects all 8 corners through projection * view, divides by clip-space w, and takes the min/max of the resulting NDC x/y, clamped to [-1,1]. A corner behind the eye (w <= 0) is skipped rather than let it fold the bounding box onto itself, so an object crossing the near plane still resolves from whichever corners are in front of the camera. A box with no corner in front of the camera at all returns the full-screen Scissor: there is nothing visible to clip to, and the caller almost certainly should not be drawing it this frame regardless of what the scissor rect says.
