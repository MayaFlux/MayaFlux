22/**
23 * @class Expanse
24 * @brief A defined region of space with a containment test and crossing actions.
25 *
26 * An Expanse is an extent, not a point. It answers whether a world position
27 * lies within it, and fires actions when an entity crosses its edge: one on
28 * entry, one on exit, and optionally one on every commit while inside. The
29 * Fabric evaluates the Expanse against every indexed position on each commit
30 * and diffs membership against the previous commit. The Expanse does not
31 * query; the Fabric drives it, because only the Fabric sees consecutive
32 * snapshots.
33 *
34 * The region is a box, a predicate, or both. A box is plain data: it moves or
35 * resizes with set_bounds, which any influence can drive through bind_bounds.
36 * A predicate is a function from position to bool that closes over whatever
37 * state shapes the region. With both, the box is a cheap first test and both
38 * must pass. Derived classes may replace contains().
39 *
40 * Crossing actions are either a plain function of the entity id, or any number
41 * of typed actions added with add_entry, add_exit and add_inside. A typed
42 * action builds a value from the CrossingContext and hands it to its targets,
43 * so the targets in FieldTargets.hpp work here as they do for an Agent. What
44 * crossing means computationally is the action's decision.
45 *
46 * An Expanse also perceives its own contents, as a Sensor perceives its
47 * neighbourhood. add_perception sources receive a PerceptionContext centred on
48 * the region, with the occupants as the spatial results, once per evaluate and
49 * before any crossing action. Typed actions and perceptions are runtime objects
50 * and are not part of encoded state.
51 *
52 * The id is assigned at construction and is the same on every Fabric the
53 * Expanse is registered on.
54 */

249 /**
250 * @brief Add a perception of any type over the entities inside, with as many targets as are wanted.
251 * @tparam T Type the source returns; whatever the targets take.
252 * @param source Reads the value from a PerceptionContext whose position and radius
253 * are the region's centre and reach, and whose spatial results are
254 * the occupants: entity id and squared distance from the centre.
255 * @return The perception, to add its targets with Perception::add_target.
256 *
257 * Runs once per evaluate for each Fabric, before the crossing actions.
