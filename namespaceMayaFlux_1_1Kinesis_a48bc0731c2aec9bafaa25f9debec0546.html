Arithmetic centroid of the positions in interleaved vertex bytes.

Every Kakshya vertex record is 60 bytes with the position at offset 0, but any stride and offset work, so readback bytes, node vertex data and MeshData all go through here without a typed copy.

Parameters
bytesInterleaved records. A trailing partial record is ignored.
strideBytes per record.
offsetByte offset of the vec3 position within a record.
Returns
Mean position, or the zero vector when no whole record fits or stride is too small to hold a position.
