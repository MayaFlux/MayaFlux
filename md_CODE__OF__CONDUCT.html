Code of Conduct and Ethical Declarations

This project is dedicated to fostering a welcoming, safe, and open environment.

We explicitly prohibit:

  • Gatekeeping, coercion, or harassment of contributors or users.
  • Institutional appropriation of contributions.
  • Retaliation against contributors asserting their ownership and rights.

We stand firmly against the misuse of this project for:

  • Institutional moral policing,
  • Proprietary restriction,
  • Exploitative behavior.

By participating, you agree to uphold these principles.

Anti-Cooptation and Ethical Use Policy

In addition to the GPLv3 terms, the following policies apply to ensure the free and fair use of this project:

  • No Institutional Claiming: No institution, organization, or employer may claim exclusive ownership, control, or proprietary rights over this project, beyond the rights explicitly allowed by GPLv3.
  • Public Benefit Commitment: This work is intended for free, public access. It must not be incorporated into private, gated, or proprietary systems without preserving open access and proper attribution.
  • Physical Property Distinction: This software is intellectual property only. Any associated physical hardware, tools, or teaching materials created alongside this software remain under their respective creators' control unless separately and explicitly agreed upon.
  • No Forced Curriculum Integration: Attempts to forcibly incorporate this work into institutional curricula, frameworks, or policies without respecting its open-source nature and without proper attribution will be considered a violation of the project's ethical guidelines.

Attribution and Cultural Identity

The name MayaFlux is rooted in the Sanskrit concept of Māyā (माया), referring to illusion, appearance, and the dynamic shaping of reality, a foundational principle in various schools of Indian philosophy. This project embraces and reinterprets these concepts through computational and generative systems design. Majority of the namespaces within the code reinterpret a network of related Sanskrit terminology for contextual and aesthetical descriptions, including but not limited to Lila, Yantra, Vruta, Kakshya, Vidhi etc.

The usage of this term is not incidental but intentional, reflecting the philosophical and cultural heritage of its author and origin. MayaFlux was created by a South Asian developer as an extension of inherited cultural frameworks, and stands in continuity with those traditions.

Any attempt to appropriate, commercialize, or monopolize the term Maya (or MayaFlux) divorced from its cultural context, particularly by entities with no historical or cultural stake in it, will be treated as an act of erasure and misrepresentation.

This statement does not impose restrictions beyond the GPLv3 license, but serves as a cultural marker against historical patterns of appropriation and intellectual extraction.
