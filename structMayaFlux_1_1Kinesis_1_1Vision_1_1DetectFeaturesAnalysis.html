Detailed Description

Keypoint reduction, computed on GPU by the same reduction shader as TrackObjectsAnalysis (track_reduce.comp, fed previous = position so the velocity output is always zero and simply not surfaced here): centroid and bounds over every detected keypoint.

Not a CPU min/max loop over the downloaded keypoint list.
