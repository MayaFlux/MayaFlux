Fourier series of a phase.

The phase in radians is window[0]. coefs: [a0, a1, b1, a2, b2, ...] and the result is a0 + sum a_k cos(k t) + b_k sin(k t). A final a_k without its b_k is read with b_k = 0. One sine and one cosine are evaluated, the harmonics follow by rotation.
