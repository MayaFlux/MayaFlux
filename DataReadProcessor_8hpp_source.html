11/**
12 * @brief Reads selected buffer storage into owned host data, one snapshot per request.
13 *
14 * Each read_* call arms one request that runs on the next graphics cycle; the
15 * matching resolve_* call finishes it on the consuming thread, waiting for a
16 * device-local copy when one is in flight. Arm again after resolving to read
17 * every frame.
18 *
19 * Geometry: read_vertices, read_mesh. Pixels: read_host_pixels,
