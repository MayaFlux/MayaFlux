void MayaFlux::Nodes::Input::OSCNode::notify_tick ( double  value)
overrideprotectedvirtual

Fires the hooks for one delivered input.

Called only from process_input(), so every hook runs on the thread that delivers input and never on the graph thread. process_sample() only advances smoothing and does not notify. The context, previous-value and range state are therefore owned by the input thread, and Node::m_last_output by the graph thread.
