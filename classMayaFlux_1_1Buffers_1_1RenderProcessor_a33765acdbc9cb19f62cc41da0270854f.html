void MayaFlux::Buffers::RenderProcessor::record_draw ( const std::shared_ptr< VKBuffer > &  buffer)

Record the buffer's prepared geometry into a fresh secondary command buffer.

Does not advance feeds, upload geometry, or dispatch milling. Both normal processing and presentation refreshes use this operation. A buffer that has not prepared geometry yet records nothing.
