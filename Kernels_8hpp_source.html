3/**
4 * @file Kernels.hpp
5 * @brief Pure functions of a newest-first window and an array of numbers.
6 *
7 * Every kernel has the shape `double(window, coefs)` and reads the window as
8 * a newest-first span: window[0] is the current value, window[k] is k steps
9 * back. In a recursive Polynomial, window[0] is the new input and window[k]
10 * is the output k steps ago. The array is whatever the kernel documents it
11 * to be, and nothing about it is a mode: the numbers in it are the behaviour.
12 *
13 * The functions convert directly to Polynomial::CoefBufferFunction, so
14 * `Polynomial(Kinesis::Discrete::weighted_sum, mode, size, weights)` is a
15 * complete node. Read-only kernels take a const array; phasor_bank writes
16 * its phases back into the array and takes a mutable one.
17 */

128/**
129 * @brief Polynomial in several lags at once.
130 *
131 * coefs: [L, P, C...] with L lags and highest power P, then (P + 1) numbers
132 * per lag, lag-major and power-ascending. The result is the sum over lags k
133 * and powers j of C[k][j] * window[k]^j. Power zero is a constant, so a bias
134 * is C[0][0]. In a recursive Polynomial this is any nonlinear difference
135 * equation of the last L outputs: Henon is lags 0, 1, 2 with
136 * C[0][0] = 1, C[1][2] = -a, C[2][1] = b. Lags beyond the window are
137 * skipped; an array shorter than its header gives zero.
138 */
139[[nodiscard]] MAYAFLUX_API double polynomial_lags(
140 std::span<const double> window, std::span<const double> coefs) noexcept;

143 * @brief Fourier series of a phase.

287/**
288 * @brief Kuramoto oscillators pulled toward their mean phase.
289 *
290 * Each call advances every phase by its own increment plus
291 * coupling * r * sin(mean_phase - phase), where r e^(i mean_phase) is the
292 * mean of the phases on the unit circle, then returns the mean of the sines.
293 * With no coupling the group stays loose; past a critical coupling it
294 * locks, and the output grows as the phases align. Costs a few
295 * trigonometric calls per oscillator per call.
