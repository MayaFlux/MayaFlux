Detailed Description

Mutable UTF-8 text plus a byte-offset cursor.

cursor is always a codepoint boundary into text: every function below that moves or writes it goes through next_codepoint_offset(), previous_codepoint_offset(), or encode_utf8(), so nothing here ever sets it to an arbitrary byte offset.
