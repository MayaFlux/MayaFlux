Detailed Description

Time evolution of arrays of independent levels.

Every function here takes arrays of numbers and writes an array of levels. What the numbers are, and whether two entries are alike or different, is entirely their content: nothing here has a notion of grouping or agreement, and identical numbers give identical levels.
