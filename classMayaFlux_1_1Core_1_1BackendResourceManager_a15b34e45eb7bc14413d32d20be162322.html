bool MayaFlux::Core::BackendResourceManager::blit_image ( const std::shared_ptr< VKImage > &  src,
const std::shared_ptr< VKImage > &  dst,
const vk::ImageBlit &  region,
vk::Filter  filter,
bool  deferred = false 
)

Blit a region of one image into a region of another.

Scales with filter and converts between supported formats. The subresources in region choose the array layers. Both images are returned to the layout they held, or shader read when it was transient.

Parameters
srcSource image.
dstDestination image, distinct from src.
regionSubresources and offsets.
filterNearest or linear.
deferredRecord for deferred submission.
Returns
True if the blit was recorded or executed.
