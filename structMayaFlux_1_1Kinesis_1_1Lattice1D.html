Detailed Description

A regular subdivision of an AABB1D into a number of cells.

One dimensional sibling of Lattice2D and Lattice3D, in double precision. Turns a scalar into a symbol: cell_at maps a value to the cell that holds it, so a SymbolicTrajectory<Lattice1D, uint32_t> observes a scalar stream the way the 2D and 3D forms observe a moving point. Indexing is the cell coordinate itself.
