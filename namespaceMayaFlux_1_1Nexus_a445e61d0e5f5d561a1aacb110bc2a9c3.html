Target that feeds an influence of transform functions into one slot.

Parameters
opInstanceFieldOperator or MeshTransformOperator, raw pointer or shared_ptr.

The influence's value is the operator's own TransformField: for an InstanceFieldOperator a function from the slot's current transform to its next one, for a MeshTransformOperator a function from accumulated seconds to the slot's local transform.
