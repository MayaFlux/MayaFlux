MAYAFLUX_API std::shared_ptr< Kakshya::DynamicSoundStream > MayaFlux::create_ring ( double  seconds,
uint32_t  channels = 1 
)

Make a circular stream that keeps the most recent seconds of a signal.

The stream runs at the engine sample rate. Fill it with record_into and read it with taps that use lag, or copy its recent past with snapshot.

Parameters
secondsHow much of the past to keep.
channelsNumber of channels.
Returns
The ring, or nullptr if it would be empty.
