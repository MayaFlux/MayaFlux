Begin recording a numbered .vdb sequence from a volume.

Constructs a VolumeCapture on the engine scheduler and retains it. The capture spawns a GraphicsRoutine that reads one frame and suspends on a FrameDelay, so it advances on the frame clock and resumes on the thread that has command queue access. Nothing polls it and nothing drives it.

The readback stays on the graphics thread because it must. The encode is dispatched to the save task pool, so the per-frame cost on the graphics thread is the transfer alone.

For a capture the caller owns and drives itself, construct IO::VolumeCapture directly.

Parameters
volumeVolume to record.
path_patternDestination with one index field, such as "smoke.{:04}.vdb". Zero padding matters.
field_namesFields to record. Empty means all.
optionsWriter options, applied to every frame.
max_framesStop after this many frames. Zero is unbounded.
frame_intervalFrames between captures.
Returns
Capture handle for stop_volume_capture, or 0 on failure.
