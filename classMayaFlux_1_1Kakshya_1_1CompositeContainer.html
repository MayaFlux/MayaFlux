Detailed Description

Owns composite data and a bounded, independently owned window.

Elements share one CompositeLayout and remain packed in the two NDData-backed stores of CompositeArray. Insertion, slices, and traversals operate directly on that array. materialize_next() copies a bounded selection into a second CompositeArray, rebasing selected text offsets into its own text store. Each traversal owns its own position and window size.

Specializes NDDataContainer<CompositeArray> directly, not SignalSourceContainer, so it carries no ProcessingState and no DataProcessor attach/detach lifecycle. On-demand, non-recurring access goes through DataFieldTraversal<CompositeArray> instead, obtained via traverse().

Usage:

CompositeLayout layout;
layout.add_field<std::string>("name");
layout.add_field<double>("score");
CompositeContainer container(std::move(layout), 64);
auto insertion = container.insert();
auto row = insertion.append();
insertion.set_text(row, "name", "Ada");
insertion.set(row, "score", 0.95);
auto traversal = container.traverse(32);
auto scores = traversal.window_to_nddata<double>("score");
container.materialize_next();
auto first = container.get_materialized_data().at(0);
