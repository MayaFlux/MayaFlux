21/**
22 * @brief Extract outer and hole contours from a binary mask.
23 *
24 * Labels 8-connected foreground components and traces their outer boundaries
25 * with Moore neighborhood following. Enclosed 4-connected background regions
26 * are traced as holes, with parent_label naming their enclosing foreground
27 * component. Background connected to an image edge is excluded.
28 * Outer contours use Contour::no_parent (0xFFFFFFFF) as their parent_label.
29 * Each contour closes from its last point to its first. Closure follows the
30 * starting directed edge so touching branches may revisit the start pixel.
31 *
32 * Area is (absolute pixel polygon area + half the pixel perimeter + 1) divided
33 * by w * h. Perimeter is the closed polygon length in pixels.
34 *
35 * Contours with fewer than 3 points are discarded.
36 * Contours whose normalised area is below min_area are discarded after tracing.
37 * A nonzero max_contours sorts by descending area and retains at most that
38 * many contours. Outer boundaries and holes share this limit.
39 * A nonzero max_points_per_contour stops each trace at that many points;
40 * measurements use the retained polygon, including its closing edge.
41 *
42 * @param mask Single-channel float span, size must be w * h.
43 * @param w Image width in pixels.
44 * @param h Image height in pixels.
45 * @param min_area Minimum normalised area [0,1] to retain. Default 0 (no filter).
46 * @param max_contours Maximum number of contours to return. 0 means no limit.
47 * @param max_points_per_contour Maximum trace length. 0 means no caller limit.
48 * @return Contours with normalised points and area, and pixel perimeter.
49 */
50[[nodiscard]] MAYAFLUX_API std::vector<Contour> find_contours(
51 std::span<const float> mask, uint32_t w, uint32_t h,
52 float min_area = 0.0F, uint32_t max_contours = 0,
53 uint32_t max_points_per_contour = 0);
