MAYAFLUX_API std::optional< glm::vec3 > MayaFlux::Nexus::read_anchor ( const std::shared_ptr< Buffers::VKBuffer > &  buf,
const std::optional< uint32_t > &  index 
)

Anchor of a buffer's geometry in its own stored coordinates.

The position of the vertex at index, else the surface centroid, else the vertex mean for a mesh without faces. A point or path buffer reports the mean of its vertices; an InstanceNetworkBuffer, with no index, the centre of its bounds. Blocks for a ComputeMeshBuffer and for a buffer a field processor drives.

Parameters
bufBuffer to read.
indexVertex to anchor at, if any.
Returns
The anchor, or nullopt when no geometry can be read from the buffer.
