noexcept

Sum of gain * window[lag] over (lag, gain) pairs.

coefs holds pairs: coefs[2j] is a lag in samples, coefs[2j + 1] its gain. Fractional lags interpolate linearly between neighbours. A pair whose lag lies outside the window contributes nothing, so a short window simply reaches fewer taps.
