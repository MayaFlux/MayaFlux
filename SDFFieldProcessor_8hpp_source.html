9/**
10 * @class SDFFieldProcessor
11 * @brief Preprocessor that evaluates an SDF field into a corner grid on the GPU.
12 *
13 * Dispatches sdf_field.comp, which includes sdf_primitives.glsl and writes
14 * one float per grid corner into the grid buffer owned by SDFPrepProcessor.
15 * SDFMeshProcessor (flat slot) reads this buffer as its SDF input.
16 *
17 * Receives the grid buffer at construction so it can bind it without
18 * depending on the attached buffer's own storage.
19 *
20 * Push constant layout (must match sdf_field.comp exactly, 48 bytes):
21 * offset 0 vec3 bounds_min
22 * offset 12 float time
23 * offset 16 vec3 step
24 * offset 28 uint res_x
25 * offset 32 uint res_y
26 * offset 36 uint res_z
27 * offset 40 uint _pad[2]
28 *
29 * Usage — wired by ComputeMeshBuffer::setup_processors when GPU field mode
30 * is active:
31 * @code
32 * chain->add_preprocessor(m_field_processor, self);
33 * @endcode
34 */
