Sum of N sinusoidal partials, each with its own slow amplitude swell.

coefs is the array phasor_bank_state builds. Every call advances each partial's phase and swell phase by its increment and returns sum(scale * sin(phase) * (0.5 + 0.5 * sin(swell_phase))). The window is not read. An array too short for its stated count gives zero. Increments must stay below one revolution per sample.
