Detailed Description

Caller-supplied context for VisionIntent::TrackObjects.

subject_bounds narrows which tracked points count as the subject when more than one candidate is present in the raw TrackResult list, for the frame that first identifies a subject. subject_track_id continues an already-identified subject on later frames by its stable TrackResult::id instead of re-filtering by region every call, which is the case DetectFeaturesContext has no equivalent for: Keypoint carries no persistent identity at all, only TrackResult does. Not derived either way: the caller decides what the subject is, from whatever source (a previous frame's result, a UI selection, another sensor).
