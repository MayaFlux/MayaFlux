Convert hue, saturation and value to red, green and blue.

All components are in [0, 1]. Hue wraps, so 1 is the same colour as 0; saturation and value are clamped.

Parameters
hsvHue in x, saturation in y, value in z.
Returns
Red in x, green in y, blue in z.
