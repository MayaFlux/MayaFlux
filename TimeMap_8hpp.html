Detailed Description

Factories for TimeMap: seconds since a voice started in, position in recorded material out (frames for a sample stream).

Doubles keep sub-frame precision on long recordings. Positions outside the material are allowed; the reader decides what they mean. For a fixed position use constant<double, double>(position).
