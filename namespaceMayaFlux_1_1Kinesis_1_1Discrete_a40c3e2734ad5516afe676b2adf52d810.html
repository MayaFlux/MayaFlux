Kuramoto oscillators pulled toward their mean phase.

Each call advances every phase by its own increment plus coupling * r * sin(mean_phase - phase), where r e^(i mean_phase) is the mean of the phases on the unit circle, then returns the mean of the sines. With no coupling the group stays loose; past a critical coupling it locks, and the output grows as the phases align. Costs a few trigonometric calls per oscillator per call.
