8/**
9 * @class AssemblyNetwork
10 * @brief Holds hundreds or thousands of independent geometry items with no
11 * shared topology, layout, or relation to each other, rendered as
12 * one milled draw.
13 *
14 * Thin wrapper around a single AssemblyOperator, forwarding add_geometry()/
15 * remove_geometry() so the common case doesn't require reaching into
16 * create_operator<AssemblyOperator>() manually. Not a specialized operator
17 * (PathOperator/TopologyOperator/PhysicsOperator interpolate, connect, or
18 * simulate; this does none of that), and not built-in-primitive convenience:
19 * callers supply their own geometry from Kinesis generators, input, or
20 * procedural code and this just holds and draws it.
21 *
22 * Renders through the standard NetworkGeometryBuffer(network)->setup_rendering
23 * path with @c triangulate enabled: milling erases the per-item layout/
24 * topology mismatch, so a heterogeneous, arbitrarily large item count draws
25 * through exactly one RenderProcessor pass, the same way a mixed-topology
26 * PathOperator does.
27 */
