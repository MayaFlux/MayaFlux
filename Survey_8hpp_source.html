27/**
28 * @brief Bounds of a buffer's geometry in its own stored coordinates.
29 *
30 * A VolumeGridBuffer reports its lattice bounds. A NetworkGeometryBuffer reports
31 * its vertices, or those of one collection. The primary operator only knows the
32 * authored positions and the CPU updates it dispatched, so once the chain holds a
33 * GpuFieldOperator the bytes are downloaded from the device instead, as the spatial
34 * export does; before the device buffer exists the operator's bytes are used. A
35 * GeometryBuffer on a node other than a MeshWriterNode, a point or path node for

37 * VertexFieldProcessor is on the buffer. An InstanceNetworkBuffer reports the union
38 * of its slots' bounds through the slot transforms as the CPU holds them; the eight
39 * corners are placed, so a rotated slot is bounded loosely, and a transform a GPU
40 * kernel drives is not seen. Other mesh buffers are read as read_anchor reads them.
41 * Blocks for a ComputeMeshBuffer and for a buffer a field processor drives: call it
42 * from a FRAME_ACCURATE metro or the main thread, never the graphics thread.
43 *
44 * @param buf Buffer to read.
45 * @param collection Collection of a NetworkGeometryBuffer to bound, if not all of it.
